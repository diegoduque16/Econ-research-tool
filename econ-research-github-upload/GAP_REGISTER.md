## Important methodological warnings
- DiD diagnostic output is NOT a causal effect. Its pretrend and placebo numbers are screening tools only.
- Matching labels across providers is not evidence of matching concepts; compatibility requires explicit metadata.
- Economic observations require clear vintage, frequency, seasonality, units and geographic identifiers before comparison.
- The system does not automatically collect all global economic data or run nine independent AI agents.
